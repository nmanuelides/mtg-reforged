The table

Play Commander against the AI

Forge's rules engine runs every card for real: the stack, priority, triggers, combat and state-based actions. The table around it was drawn from scratch for touch.

  • One to three opponents in Commander, each with their own deck, name and avatar.
  • Undo, up to 20 steps. Take back a land, a spell on the stack or an attack. Undo while paying a cost cancels the spell.
  • Save and leave any game and continue it later. Your undo steps come along.
  • Clear combat. Arrows from attackers to whoever they attack and from blockers to what they block. Struck cards shake, spells fly to their targets.
  • Play with friends Coming soon on the same Wi-Fi: host a lobby, pick decks, fill empty seats with AI.
The workshop

Build decks from every card

A deck builder that searches like a database, prices like a store and reads like a binder.

  • Full search: name, rules text, colours, colour identity, sets, keywords, legality, mana cost, types, power, toughness, rarity and artist.
  • Import plain lists, Moxfield and ManaBox. Export to text or share.
  • Card Kingdom prices, updated daily, for regular and foil printings. One tap switches a deck to its cheapest printings.
  • Shopping list of the cards you don't own yet, ready to copy or share.
  • Commanders are picked with a star, and imports find the commander on their own.
The binder

Your collection, printing by printing

  • Quick price check: type any card in the search on the home screen to see it big with its cheapest Card Kingdom price, plus every printing and what each one costs.
  • Every printing and finish is its own entry, so a foil and a regular copy of the same card are counted apart.
  • Foil cards shine, with a holographic sweep across the art.
  • Profiles: your name and avatar, and avatars for each opponent, cropped from a photo or any card's art.
Offline

Play without a connection

Download the card pictures ahead of time, and play, browse and build decks anywhere: on a plane, on the subway, or with bad Wi-Fi.

  • Every card in your decks, plus the preconstructed ones you play against: everything you'll see in a match. About 21,600 pictures, up to 1.9 GB.
  • Every card in Magic: one printing of each, to browse the whole card pool and build decks offline. About 33,900 pictures, up to 2.9 GB.
  • Over Wi-Fi only, in the background, so it never eats your mobile data.
  • No need to finish in one go. If the download stops halfway, start it again later and it picks up where it left off, skipping the pictures you already have.
Backup & restore

Your cards follow you

Save your collection and your decks to Google Drive, and bring them back on any phone, tablet or PC.

  • One tap to Google Drive. Your collection and the decks you made are saved in your own Drive.
  • Restore on any device signed in with the same Google account.
  • Pick the backup to bring back: each one shows when it was made and how many cards and decks it holds.
  • No Google account needed: save the backup as a file and open it on another device.

Built on Forge

Forge

Every rule, every card script and every AI opponent in MTG Reforged comes from Forge, the open-source Magic: The Gathering engine its community has been building for more than fifteen years.

The engine is Forge's own code, merged from Card-Forge/forge every day and compiled without changes, so new cards and rules fixes arrive as soon as Forge has them.

Everything around it is MTG Reforged's own work. Besides a new interface designed for touch and mouse, it adds a lot that Forge doesn't have: undo up to 20 steps that survives saving, a collection that tracks every printing and foil, daily Card Kingdom prices, deck imports from Moxfield and ManaBox, cheapest-printing and shopping lists, Google Drive backup, profiles and avatars, offline card images, combat arrows and impact animations, and apps that update themselves.

Thank you to everyone who has written for Forge. Like Forge, MTG Reforged is free software under the GNU GPL v3.

Android phones

Best played on a tablet

A Commander table with four players needs room. On a phone the cards and controls get too small to play comfortably.

A phone is still a great companion: look up any card and its price, keep your collection up to date and build decks wherever you are, then play them on your tablet or PC.
Installing on Windows

Windows may warn you about the installer

When you open the installer, Windows may show a blue screen saying “Windows protected your PC”. Windows shows it for apps from independent developers that haven't paid for a code-signing certificate. It doesn't mean anything harmful was found.
